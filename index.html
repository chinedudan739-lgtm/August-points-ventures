ABOUT US

Building Products for Everyday Needs

August Points Ventures is a Lagos-based business focused on the production and supply of cosmetics, cleaning products and personal-care and hospitality kits.

Led by Augustine Chukwunyere, Chief Executive Officer / Managing Director, the company serves individuals, businesses and hospitality establishments with a range of practical everyday products.
